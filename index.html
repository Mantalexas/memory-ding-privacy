Memory Ding

Privacy Policy — Last updated September 20, 2026

Memory Ding is developed and published by Mantas Žemeckas.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Memory Ding.

Advertising

Memory Ding uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google.

The game displays banner advertisements and may offer optional rewarded advertisements. Players may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as receiving additional in-game currency. Watching rewarded advertisements is optional.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, interactions with advertisements, diagnostic information, and other identifiers or technical information needed to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on the user's location, consent choices, age-related requirements, app configuration, and applicable law.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Memory Ding uses Google's consent system to request privacy and advertising choices before requesting advertisements that require consent.

Users may be able to choose between available advertising and privacy options presented by Google. Memory Ding will provide access to privacy and advertising choices from within the game when required, allowing users to review or change their consent choices.

Information Stored by the Game

Memory Ding stores game-related information locally on the user's device, such as settings, high scores, selected and unlocked cosmetic items, in-game currency, and other gameplay preferences or progress.

This locally stored information is used to operate the game and remember the player's settings and progress. The developer does not currently operate a Memory Ding user-account system or game server that receives this local save information.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its application data may remove locally stored game information.

Third-Party Services

Memory Ding uses Google AdMob and related Google advertising and consent technologies.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations. Their data handling practices are controlled by the respective service providers.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising, measure advertisement performance, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with legal obligations, and provide related advertising services.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to limit data handling by Memory Ding to what is necessary for the game's functionality and integrated services.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the security practices of those service providers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally by Memory Ding remains on the user's device until it is overwritten, application data is cleared, or the game is uninstalled.

Memory Ding does not currently provide user accounts or maintain a developer-operated database containing player profiles.

Data processed by Google or other advertising providers is retained and deleted according to those providers' respective policies and applicable law.

For privacy questions concerning Memory Ding or requests relating to information directly controlled by the developer, users may contact the developer using the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Memory Ding is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a developer-operated account or server.

Advertising and consent behavior may be adjusted where required by applicable laws, platform policies, and the age group selected for distribution of the game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Memory Ding's features, advertising services, data practices, or legal requirements change. Changes will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
